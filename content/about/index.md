I'm a full-stack research software engineer with 7+ years of experience building learning technologies and real-time multimodal data analytics platforms. I'm a lead system engineer at ICONS (formerly the [Institute for Software Integrated Systems](https://www.isis.vanderbilt.edu)) at Vanderbilt University. I work with the [Open-Ended Learning Environments lab](https://wp0.vanderbilt.edu/oele/) and the NSF [AI Institute for Engaged Learning](https://engageai.org/).

## What I work on

**AI tutoring at scale.** I created and lead Betty's Math, a math assessment and AI-tutoring platform embedded in the OECD's PILA. It is deployed in a randomized controlled trial across hundreds of schools in France, Germany, the Slovak Republic and Latvia. I built its LLM tutor and the context it reasons over, the LLM pipelines that translate content for four countries and build concept knowledge graphs, and the research layer: RCT sequencing, xAPI logging and a real-time teacher dashboard.

**Multimodal classroom data.** I designed and lead [SyncFlow](https://github.com/oele-isis-vanderbilt/SyncFlow), an open-source Rust/Next.js/WebRTC platform that sustains 200 concurrent streams at about 200 ms latency. It has archived 400+ hours of data for classroom studies in Indiana, Tennessee and North Carolina. Before that, I co-developed [ChimeraPy](https://github.com/ChimeraPy), a distributed streaming framework for real-time multimodal data. I wrote about that journey in [*Days of Collection, Days of Despair*](/blog/days-of-collection-days-of-despair/). I'm now building real-time AI feedback for classrooms, including on-device LLM summarization of live transcripts on NVIDIA Jetson.

**Earlier work.** From 2019 to 2022 I was a core contributor to [DeepForge](https://github.com/deepforge-dev/deepforge), a visual environment for deep learning. I led development of [GMSO](https://github.com/mosdef-hub/gmso) in the MoSDeF molecular simulation ecosystem and built design tools for DARPA's Symbiotic Design for CPS program. I've also worked on surrogate models and deep active learning for engineering design.

See my [publications](/publications/) or [CV](/cv/) for more.

## Background

I'm originally from [Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal). I'm currently pursuing a part-time M.S. in Data Science at Vanderbilt. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from [Southern Illinois University Carbondale](https://siu.edu) in 2019, where I was a graduate research assistant, and my B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from the [Institute of Engineering, Thapathali Campus](https://www.tcioe.edu.np/), Tribhuvan University.
