I'm a full-stack research software engineer with 7+ years of experience building learning technologies and real-time multimodal analytics platforms. I'm a lead system engineer at ICONS (formerly the [Institute for Software Integrated Systems](https://www.isis.vanderbilt.edu)) at Vanderbilt University, where I work with the [Open-Ended Learning Environments lab](https://wp0.vanderbilt.edu/oele/) and the NSF [AI Institute for Engaged Learning](https://engageai.org/).

I created and lead **Betty's Math**, an assessment and AI-tutoring platform used in an OECD randomized controlled trial across hundreds of schools in four European countries. I also lead **[SyncFlow](https://github.com/oele-isis-vanderbilt/SyncFlow)**, an open-source platform that has captured 400+ hours of multimodal classroom data in U.S. studies. I like taking ideas from learning scientists, teachers and international partners and turning them into dependable research software.
