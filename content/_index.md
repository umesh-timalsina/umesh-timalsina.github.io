I'm a research engineer at the [Institute for Software Integrated Systems](https://www.isis.vanderbilt.edu) at Vanderbilt University. I work with the [Open-Ended Learning Environments (OELE) lab](https://wp0.vanderbilt.edu/oele/) on the multimodal learning analytics strand of the NSF [EngageAI Institute](https://engageai.org/).

My work centers on the infrastructure behind classroom research: systems that capture audio, video, screen and log data from many students at once, keep it on a single timeline, and make it usable for analysis. I lead development of [SyncFlow](https://github.com/oele-isis-vanderbilt/SyncFlow), which has been deployed in classroom studies.
